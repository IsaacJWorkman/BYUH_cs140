President Russell Marion Nelson Sr.

Prophet, seer, revelator, healer, man of excellent

President Nelson was born in Salt Lake City, Utah. After growing up he attended the Medical school at the University of Utah. As part his medical work he served in the Army Medial corps. He earned an MD and a PHD and was on the team that developed open heart surgery via the cardiopulmonary bypass machine.

Russell M. Nelson served as an apostle of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints for many many years before being called by the Lord to be the President of the church. As president he taught man important and relevant things and started the construction of hundreds of temples. His teachings included changes to the policy of the church and many witnesses of doctrine that help us to focus on Christ and gathering the children of God on both sides of the veil.

"But in coming days, it will not be possible to survive spiritually without the guiding, directing, comforting, and constant influence of the Holy Ghost."

— Revelation for the Church, Revelation for Our Lives; April 2018
