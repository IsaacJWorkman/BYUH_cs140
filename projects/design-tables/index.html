Beyblade:

Inspired by a traditional Japanese top-spinning game, the Beyblade toys and manga series were released together in 1999. There have been 4 different generations of Beyblade toys and Animated series: Original, Metal Saga, Burst Era, Beyblade X. Beyblade X has become the most popular and best selling version of Beyblades with parts that can be exchanged to make custom combinations. It has developed a competitive scene stretching across the globe.
